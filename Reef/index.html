The Mission

PROTECTING THE REEFS

Coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor — yet support 25% of all marine life. Reef Reimagined is a citizen-science platform that turns every dive, snorkel, and underwater photograph into data that drives reef restoration, fisheries management, and invasive-species response.

Built on the methodology of the Reef Environmental Education Foundation (REEF), the platform connects volunteer divers to the largest marine-life sighting database on Earth — and to the AI tools that turn observations into action.
